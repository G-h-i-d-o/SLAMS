# Privacy Notice

## What we collect

- **Account data**: your email, full name, and role.
- **Operational data**: SLA metrics, configurations, and audit logs you create.
- **Technical data**: IP address (in server logs), browser user-agent.

## Why we collect it

- To authenticate you and authorize your access.
- To provide the SLA Management service.
- To maintain a security and audit trail.

## Who we share it with

- **Supabase** — database, authentication, storage.
- **Netlify** — hosting and serverless functions.
- **Sentry** — error tracking (only if enabled).
- **Cloudflare** — security (only if Turnstile is enabled).

We do **not** sell your data.

## How long we keep it

- Account data: until you or an admin deletes your account.
- Operational data: per the retention policy in `docs/COMPLIANCE.md`.
- Audit logs: 3 years.

## Your rights

- Access, rectification, erasure, portability, objection.
- Contact: `<your-support-email>`.

## Cookies

We use one essential cookie for the session (Supabase Auth). No tracking cookies.
